Data selection, offline conversion and training
Panda to UR5e | source-only VLA pilot | 2
Source materials: 50 original task demonstrations, 7,638 recorded steps. Reserve five source
episodes. Inspect the remaining 45 with four-pose B IK and unintended arm-collision probes; 35
pass. Select the shortest eligible episode and then diversify initial plate positions.
Selected source episodes: demo_5, demo_38, demo_26. They contain 396 original steps. Static
per-frame checks retain 189 training anchors. Images and states of hypothetical B poses are
rendered from A object records without target physics stepping.
Pose conversion uses full EEF orientation and position, continuous warm-start IK, joint limits,
static arm-contact checks, a fixed camera mount calibration and a source-compatible
proprioception frame. OSC actions share world-frame units; the first action is corrected for
residual TCP position error. Future chunk labels retain original source Cartesian commands.
These are kinematic pseudo-labels, not verified successful B trajectories.
The complete 450M-parameter SmolVLA checkpoint is loaded strictly from lerobot/smolvla_libero
revision a24f1f380461b665158d52acd140152c79e8b36d. The visual/language backbone and
original model weights are frozen. Rank-8 LoRA updates 334,592 parameters in action-expert
Q/V and action projections.
Training: 100 updates, batch four, LR 1e-4, action chunks of fifty at 20 Hz. Measured training
wall time: 17.56 seconds, including model loading. Peak PyTorch allocated memory: 1.59 GiB.
Evaluation executes five predicted actions before replanning, with ten flow steps per query. Two
reserved starts per condition have 200-step maximum horizons.
Images are rendered at 256px, rotated by the official LIBERO 180-degree convention, and
passed through the saved VLA image preprocessing. Normalization uses the exact saved source
checkpoint tensors. An earlier run with a missing image rotation is archived locally and excluded
from final comparison.

Observed limitations and review evidence
Panda to UR5e | source-only VLA pilot | 3
The source VLA succeeded on demo_45 but failed on demo_46. A successful original human
record does not imply the pretrained VLA will succeed. A 1/2 baseline cannot substantiate a
reliable competent-source transfer claim.
Both adapted B cases moved the plate closer to the goal, but remained outside native success.
Offline geometry and source object motion do not supply target friction, pushing-force or
contact-timing supervision. The coarse source filter and tiny adapter run leave these issues
unresolved.
Other limits: only three selected source episodes; 100 updates; no held-out source validation for
checkpoint selection; no random-subset comparison; only two final starts; incomplete dynamic
feasibility of future action chunks. Repeated source scenes were used while correcting a
documented input-contract bug, so this is an engineering pilot, not a benchmark result.
The English comparison movies display A, unchanged B and adapted B together for both starts,
including the failed source case. offline_source_processing.mp4 distinguishes original A records
from hypothetical B kinematic rendering. Raw final rollout movies and per-seed metrics
accompany the report.
